# Usage Guide

## Purpose and Audience

This template is for university students completing research, writing, referencing, or presentation work under course requirements. It is useful for students who want staged, checkable work, traceable evidence, and Codex assistance. It does not assume a particular subject or assignment type.

## Prepare for Each Assignment

Prepare these materials separately for every assignment:

- Assignment Brief;
- Rubric or Marking Scheme;
- relevant Lecture Notes;
- instructor instructions, required sources, citation and formatting rules;
- your assigned section, word count, and deadline if provided;
- current draft and sources already found, if any.

If a key requirement is missing, clarify it first. Do not let Codex guess.

## Add It to Obsidian

1. Download or clone the project.
2. Copy the project folder into your own Vault. Check the destination first to avoid overwriting a note with the same name.
3. Open or refresh the Vault in Obsidian and check Markdown links.
4. In Codex, provide the exact local Vault path and only the files needed for the current task. A public repository template cannot automatically read local files.
5. Copy a prompt from `prompts/` that fits the current stage. Provide only necessary materials; do not ask Codex to scan your whole computer or Vault.

## Suggested Assignment Sequence

1. Read the current assignment materials and extract the task, deliverables, word count, rubric criteria, citation style, and source limits.
2. Map each rubric item to course concepts, claims to demonstrate, source evidence, and the relevant section. Mark lecture concepts “not applicable” when appropriate.
3. Search for a small set of relevant sources and record queries, links, DOIs, authors, years, and reading status.
4. Track source existence, bibliographic accuracy, and claim support as separate checks.
5. Record evidence locations, limitations, and scope for core sources. Label abstract-only or metadata-only review honestly.
6. Draft only the requested section or paragraphs and explain how evidence relates to each claim.
7. Follow your institution's current official citation guide and the instructor's assignment requirements. Do not apply TAR UMT rules automatically at another institution.
8. Review against the rubric, then personally check full text, data, formatting, academic integrity, and submission requirements.

## Verify Sources and Data

A source's existence does not prove that its bibliographic details are accurate. Accurate metadata does not prove that its content supports your claim. Prefer official journal, publisher, or institutional pages, DOI registration records, and the full text where possible. Search results, abstracts, and metadata each have limitations.

For quantitative or company data, record the data name and value, report title/year, link, page/table, units/currency/period, and any formula, inputs, and result. If the primary source or exact location cannot be found, mark it “to be confirmed”; do not invent it.

## Privacy and External Services

- Give Codex only the files required for the current task.
- Before online queries, confirm that course materials and assignment content may be sent to external services.
- Send only necessary metadata for DOI lookups; do not upload a complete assignment or body text.
- Do not put API keys, tokens, passwords, student IDs, or other people's information in prompts, public repositories, or logs.
- Do not run scripts from unknown sources. Inspect the target and impact before any installation or write operation.

## Manual Responsibilities

Codex cannot replace full-text reading, academic judgment, confirmation of institutional policies, or final submission checks. You should confirm that:

- each important claim is supported by locatable evidence in its source;
- in-text citations and references correspond and follow course requirements;
- facts, data, units, periods, and calculations are accurate;
- the work follows your institution's AI, privacy, and academic-integrity policies;
- the final file opens correctly, is complete, and is submitted on time.
