# 最终审核

## 华文指令

请只根据我提供的本次作业材料逐项审核，不虚构分数、来源或已完成的检查。

**我会提供：** 最终草稿、Assignment Brief、Rubric、Lecture Notes、老师要求及相关来源/数据记录。

**请先读取：** 本次作业文件，以及 [`详细流程 reference`](../skills/assignment-research-skills/references/完整工作流程.md) 中最终 Rubric 审核规则。按作业规模选择精简或完整追踪，不要求简单作业建立复杂来源台账。

**请完成：** Rubric 每一项单独输出一行，至少包含：评分项（沿用原编号，如有）、状态（达成/部分达成/缺失/待确认）、关联论点或课程概念、来源和原文证据位置、正文位置、缺口及下一步。分别记录来源是否找到/存在性、书目信息准确性、实际阅读范围和具体主张支持（直接/部分/间接/不支持/待确认）。对关键数字核对原始科目、期间、单位、币种、Company/Group 口径、成品数字、差额和第二次独立复核。区分来源事实、一般理论、作者推论和建议；检查商业建议是否有公司特定证据及替代方案比较。检查任务覆盖、字数、结构、适用引用规则、英文和提交要求。

**禁止：** 不用整体评价替代逐项结果；无对应证据不标达成；不把摘要/元数据、DOI 存在或主题相关说成全文核验或论点支持；不把一般理论直接当作公司决策证明；不混用科目及 Company/Group 数字；不报告未执行的检查为已完成；没有明确评分依据时不虚构分数；不改写整篇文章，除非我明确要求。免费查询达到限额或失败时停止，不切换付费服务、不索取 API Key。

**完成后报告：** 逐项状态、证据与正文位置、优先修正项、未核实或未执行事项，以及人工后续清单。

## English Prompt

Review only against the current assignment materials I provide. Do not invent a grade, source, or completed check.

**I will provide:** the final draft, Assignment Brief, Rubric, Lecture Notes, instructor requirements, and relevant source/data records.

**Read first:** these current materials and the [detailed workflow reference](../skills/assignment-research-skills/references/完整工作流程.md)'s final Rubric review rules. Choose compact or full tracking for the task; a simple assignment does not require a complex source ledger.

**Do:** report every Rubric criterion as a separate row with its original ID when present, status (met/partly met/missing/to be confirmed), related claim or concept, source and original-text evidence location, document location, gap, and next action. Keep source discovery/existence, bibliographic accuracy, reading scope, and claim support (direct/partial/indirect/not supported/to be confirmed) separate. Check material figures against the original line item, period, units, currency, Company/Group scope, assignment value, discrepancy, and independent second check. Distinguish source facts, general theory, author inference, and recommendation; assess whether business recommendations have company-specific evidence and compare alternatives. Review task coverage, word count, structure, applicable citation requirements, English, and submission rules.

**Do not:** replace criterion-level results with an overall evaluation; mark an unsupported criterion as met; treat an abstract/metadata, existing DOI, or topical match as full-text verification or claim support; treat general theory as proof of a company decision; mix line items or Company/Group figures; report unchecked work as complete; invent a grade without an explicit basis; or rewrite the whole paper unless asked. Stop free queries at limits or failure; never fall back to paid services or request an API key.

**Report:** criterion-level results, evidence and document locations, highest-priority revisions, unchecked or unverified items, and manual follow-up.
