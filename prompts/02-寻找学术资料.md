# 寻找学术资料

## 华文指令

请根据我提供的 Assignment 要求，为指定论点寻找少量相关学术和一手来源。

**我会提供：** Brief、Rubric、课程资料、要支持的论点、允许的数据库/来源类型和检索范围。

**请先读取：** 当前作业要求、相关课程概念及来源限制。

**请完成：** 说明检索式和平台；提供可复查来源链接；分别核对来源存在性、标题/完整作者及顺序/出版物/年份/DOI；标注全文、摘要、元数据或未核实状态；说明证据位置、适用范围、局限和可支持的具体论点。

**禁止：** 不编造来源、DOI、作者、年份、页码或研究结果；不把标题/摘要/元数据匹配说成全文支持；不把相关性写成因果关系；未经确认不发送敏感资料给外部服务。

**完成后报告：** 每个来源的三项核查状态、阅读范围、支持论点的证据位置、局限、检索日期及待确认事项。

## English Prompt

Find a small number of relevant academic and primary sources for the specific claims in the assignment materials I provide.

**I will provide:** the Brief, Rubric, course materials, claims to support, allowed databases/source types, and search scope.

**Read first:** the current assignment requirements, relevant course concepts, and source restrictions.

**Do:** state search queries and platforms; provide checkable source links; separately verify source existence and the title, complete author list/order, publication, year, and DOI; label full-text, abstract-only, metadata-only, or unverified reading; and explain evidence locations, scope, limitations, and the specific claims each source may support.

**Do not:** invent sources, DOIs, authors, years, page numbers, or findings; describe a title, abstract, or metadata match as full-text support; turn correlation into causation; or send sensitive information to external services without confirmation.

**Report:** the three verification statuses for each source, reading scope, evidence location, limitations, search date, and unresolved questions.
