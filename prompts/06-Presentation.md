# Presentation

## 华文指令

请根据我提供的 Presentation 作业要求，检查或协助制定指定部分，不自动制作完整幻灯片。

**我会提供：** Brief、Rubric、Lecture Notes、演讲时长、幻灯片限制、听众、语言、来源和我负责的内容。

**请先读取：** 本次作业要求、评分标准及讲义中相关概念。

**请完成：** 提出符合时长和评分标准的结构；每页聚焦一个主要信息；将事实、数据和图表对应到可核实来源；检查文内/幻灯片引用和参考页；必要时为我负责部分提供讲稿要点。

**禁止：** 不编造案例、数据或来源；不将完整作业或限制资料上传到未获批准的服务；不忽略图片版权或来源标示；不擅自制作整套文件。

**完成后报告：** 检查/起草范围、页序与时间分配、证据来源、待核实事项和本人需要完成的部分。

## English Prompt

Review or plan only the presentation portion I specify, based on the assignment requirements. Do not automatically create a complete slide deck.

**I will provide:** the Brief, Rubric, Lecture Notes, presentation duration, slide limit, audience, language, sources, and the content I am responsible for.

**Read first:** the current assignment requirements, rubric, and relevant course concepts.

**Do:** propose a structure that fits the time and criteria; focus each slide on one main message; map facts, data, and charts to verifiable sources; check slide citations and the reference slide; and provide speaker-note points for my assigned section if needed.

**Do not:** invent examples, data, or sources; upload a complete assignment or restricted material to an unapproved service; omit image-credit requirements; or create the full deck without being asked.

**Report:** the reviewed/drafted scope, slide order and timing, evidence sources, unresolved items, and what I need to complete.
