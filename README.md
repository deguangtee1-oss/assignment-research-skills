# 大学作业研究助手

**Assignment Research Skills**

## 项目背景

本项目由一名 TAR UMT（Tunku Abdul Rahman University of Management and Technology）Business Administration 学生，根据个人过往大学 Assignment 经验整理而成。它把学术资料检索、文献整理、学术写作、APA 7 引用核查、数据与图表、Presentation 和 Codex 指令整理成可参考、可自行修改的工作流程。

## 重要声明

- 这是学生个人制作的非官方项目，不是 TAR UMT 官方项目，不代表学校立场，也不表示学校认可或背书。
- 本项目是持续改进中的个人学习资源和工作流程模板，不是经过完整验证的正式成品。
- 不同大学、科目、老师和 Assignment 的要求可能不同。始终以你当前的 Assignment Brief、Rubric、Lecture Notes 和老师最新指示为准。
- AI 和 Codex 可能出错、误解要求或生成虚构参考文献。你必须自行核实来源、数据、引用及最终提交内容。
- 你有责任遵守所在机构的学术诚信、AI 使用和资料保密规定。
- 本项目不保证成绩、资料完全正确或适用于所有环境；不能代替你的学习、判断和人工检查。

## 这项目能做什么

它提供可复制的 Codex 指令和一套分阶段流程，帮助你拆解作业要求、检索和记录来源、组织证据、起草指定部分、检查引用与数据，以及对照 Rubric 做最终审核。

## 这项目不做什么

它不会自动读取你电脑或 Obsidian Vault 的资料，不会替你核实未提供的来源，也不能单靠 DOI、标题匹配或格式检查证明论文支持某论点。它不保证成绩，也不是自动完成作业的系统。

## 从零开始

1. 安装并打开 Obsidian；本项目不要求特定插件。
2. 获取 Codex，并确认你能在 Codex 对话中提供本机文件或明确的文件路径。
3. 下载或克隆本项目后，把项目文件夹复制到自己的 Obsidian Vault 中。公开仓库模板不会自动读取你的电脑文件。
4. 在 Codex 中提供自己的 Vault 本地路径；不要让 Codex 扫描整个电脑。
5. 每次作业准备 Assignment Brief、Rubric/Marking Scheme、Lecture Notes、老师补充指示及当前草稿（如有）。
6. 从 `prompts/` 选择适合当前阶段的指令；先任务拆解，再分阶段推进。
7. 按 `docs/完整工作流程.md` 记录来源、论点、课程概念、数据位置、限制和待确认问题。
8. 提交前人工核对原始来源、引用、数据、格式、老师要求和学校 AI 政策。

## 按自己的学校修改

在复制后的版本中，把学校 APA 指南、引用规则、课程政策和本地路径换成你自己学校当前适用的信息。不要把 TAR UMT 或其他学校的规则直接当作你课程的规则。若老师对某次作业给出不同规定，记录该次要求并优先执行。

## 资料与隐私

不要把整个 Vault 上传或复制到公开仓库。仅向 Codex 提供当前任务所需文件。不要把未发表作业、个人资料、同学资料、受限讲义、Rubric、考试资料、API Key、Token、密码或 Cookie 发给不可信服务。在线元数据查询只发送完成查询所需的最少信息，并先确认允许外发。

## English

### Project Background

This project was organized by a TAR UMT (Tunku Abdul Rahman University of Management and Technology) Business Administration student based on personal experience with university assignments. It brings together workflows for academic research, literature organization, academic writing, APA 7 referencing, data and charts, presentations, and Codex prompts.

### Important Disclaimer

- This is an independent student project. It is not an official TAR UMT project, does not represent the university's position, and does not imply its approval or endorsement.
- This is an evolving personal learning resource and workflow template, not a fully validated product.
- Requirements vary across institutions, courses, instructors, and assignments. Always follow your current Assignment Brief, Rubric, Lecture Notes, and the instructor's latest instructions.
- AI and Codex can make mistakes, misunderstand requirements, or fabricate references. You must verify sources, data, citations, and your final submission.
- You are responsible for following your institution's academic integrity, AI-use, and confidentiality policies.
- This project guarantees neither grades nor complete accuracy or suitability for every environment. It does not replace your learning, judgment, or manual review.

### What It Does and Does Not Do

It provides copyable Codex prompts and a staged workflow for interpreting assignment requirements, recording sources, organizing evidence, drafting a selected section, checking citations and data, and reviewing against a rubric.

It does not automatically read files on your computer or in your Obsidian Vault. It cannot verify sources that you have not provided, and DOI matches or formatting checks alone cannot prove that a paper supports a claim. It does not guarantee grades or complete assignments without your participation.

### Quick Start

1. Install and open Obsidian; no specific plugin is required.
2. Obtain Codex and confirm that you can provide local files or explicit paths in a Codex conversation.
3. Copy this project folder into your own Obsidian Vault. A public repository template cannot automatically read files on your computer.
4. Give Codex your own local Vault path; do not ask it to scan your whole computer.
5. Prepare the Assignment Brief, Rubric/Marking Scheme, Lecture Notes, instructor instructions, and current draft if available.
6. Select a prompt from `prompts/`; begin with requirement analysis and proceed in stages.
7. Use `docs/完整工作流程.md#english` to record sources, claims, course concepts, data locations, limitations, and unresolved questions.
8. Manually check primary sources, citations, data, formatting, instructor requirements, and your institution's AI policy before submission.

### Adapt It to Your Institution

Replace the school APA guide, citation rules, course policies, and local paths in your copied version with the requirements applicable at your institution. Do not treat TAR UMT or another institution's rules as universal. If an instructor gives different rules for a specific assignment, record and follow those requirements for that assignment.

### Data and Privacy

Do not upload or copy your whole Vault into a public repository. Give Codex only the files needed for the current task. Do not send unpublished work, personal data, classmates' information, restricted lecture notes, rubrics, exam material, API keys, tokens, passwords, or cookies to untrusted services. Send only the minimum information needed for online metadata queries, and first confirm that external sharing is allowed.

### Project Status

- **Organized and ready to try:** assignment planning, rubric mapping, source-verification records, evidence matrices, staged writing, final review, and bilingual Codex prompt templates.
- **Not validated:** the templates have not been systematically tested across institutions, courses, Codex versions, or real assignments. Users must review results.
- **Not distributed:** personal installation records, historical assignments and course files, and the author's insufficiently validated citation-audit scripts.
- **Known limitations:** metadata existence or correct formatting does not prove that source content supports a claim; this workflow cannot determine every academic or legal requirement.
