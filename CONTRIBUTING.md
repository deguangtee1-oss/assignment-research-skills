# 贡献指南

## 华文

欢迎提交问题、纠错和改进建议。本项目仍在持续整理，提交内容不代表 TAR UMT 或任何院校的官方意见。

贡献前请：

1. 说明建议解决的实际问题，并提供可公开核查的依据。
2. 不提交真实作业、个人或同学资料、未公开讲义、Rubric、考试材料、评分报告或凭证。
3. 不添加未经核实的参考文献、DOI、页码、数据或学校政策。
4. 学校或教师规则必须注明适用范围和来源，不得推广成所有课程的通用规则。
5. 若改动华文文档，也请更新对应英文版本；英文应忠实保留限制和免责声明。
6. 不把格式检查或元数据匹配描述成对论文内容的验证。
7. 在许可证尚未确定前，不假设贡献或项目内容已获再分发授权。

## English

Issues, corrections, and improvement proposals are welcome. This project is still evolving; contributions do not represent TAR UMT or any institution.

Before contributing:

1. Explain the practical problem and provide evidence that can be checked publicly.
2. Do not submit real assignments, personal or classmates' data, unpublished lecture notes, rubrics, exam materials, grading reports, or credentials.
3. Do not add unverified references, DOIs, page numbers, data, or institutional policies.
4. State the scope and source of any institutional or instructor rule; do not present it as universal.
5. When changing a Chinese document, update its English counterpart. Preserve limitations and disclaimers faithfully.
6. Do not describe formatting checks or metadata matches as verification of a paper's content.
7. While the license remains undecided, do not assume contributions or project content are authorized for redistribution.
