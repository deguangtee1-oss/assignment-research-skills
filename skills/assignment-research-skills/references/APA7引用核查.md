# APA 7 引用检查

## 华文指令

请检查当前作业的引用与参考文献。先确定本次作业适用的引用规则。

**我会提供：** 作业草稿、References、学校当前官方引用指南链接或文件、老师对本次作业的额外要求，以及允许进行在线查询的范围。

**请先读取：** 本次老师的引用指示；若没有指定格式，再读取我学校当前官方指南。不要默认套用模板作者学校的规则。

**请完成：** 检查文内引用与 References 的双向对应；核对可见的作者、年份、标题、出版信息和 DOI；标注重复、缺项、不一致和需要人工检查项；按实际适用指南检查格式。分别报告来源存在性、书目信息准确性和论点支持状态。

**禁止：** 不把 AI 生成的参考文献直接当成真实来源；不虚构或静默“修复”字段；不宣称 DOI 或格式正确证明论文支持论点；未经确认不向外部服务发送完整作业或正文。

**完成后报告：** 采用的指南和版本/日期、逐项问题与位置、核查链接、未核实事项及需要人工阅读的内容。

## English Prompt

Check citations and references in my current assignment. First determine which citation rules apply.

**I will provide:** the assignment draft, reference list, a link or file for my institution's current official citation guide, any additional instructor requirements for this assignment, and the scope allowed for online queries.

**Read first:** the instructor's citation instructions for this assignment. If no style is specified, consult my institution's current official guide. Do not assume the template author's institutional rules apply to me.

**Do:** check two-way correspondence between in-text citations and references; verify visible authors, years, titles, publication details, and DOIs; flag duplicates, missing or inconsistent fields, and items requiring manual review; apply the relevant guide. Report source existence, bibliographic accuracy, and claim support separately.

**Do not:** treat AI-generated references as real without verification; invent or silently “repair” fields; claim a correct DOI or format proves that a paper supports a claim; or send a complete assignment or body text to an external service without confirmation.

**Report:** the guide and version/date used, each issue and location, verification links, unverified items, and content requiring full-text review.
