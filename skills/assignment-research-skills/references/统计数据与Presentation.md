# 统计数据与 Presentation

## 中文

### 定量资料与统计

仅处理当前 Assignment 要求的变量、群体、时间范围和问题。先确认数据定义、来源、抽样或统计期间及限制。不得把描述统计、相关关系或未经核实的数字写成因果结论。

记录数据名称与数值、原始来源链接、报告名称与年度、页码/表格/章节、单位、币种和期间。计算要记录公式、输入、处理方式和结果，并说明四舍五入。披露缺失、异常或口径不一致数据；不得静默填补或混合不同单位、期间和定义。

图表用于清楚表达比较或趋势。标记标题、轴、单位、期间、数据来源；避免误导性比例、截断坐标轴或省略重要背景。说明图表能反映和不能反映什么。统计方法应符合本次作业和课程内容；不确定时询问，不自行引入复杂方法。

### Presentation

先确认听众、演讲时间、幻灯片限制、分工和 Rubric。每页聚焦一个主要信息，安排连贯叙事；把事实、数字和图表对应到可核实来源，并检查幻灯片引用与参考页。只处理用户要求部分；未获明确要求不制作整套幻灯片或代替用户提交。图片、图表和案例应注明来源并遵守授权与课程规则。

## English

### Quantitative information and statistics

Work only with the variables, population, period, and question required by the current assignment. Confirm data definitions, source, sample or reporting period, and limitations. Do not present descriptive statistics, correlations, or unchecked figures as causal findings.

Record data names and values, primary-source links, report title/year, page/table/section, units, currency, and period. For calculations, keep the formula, inputs, treatment, and result; state rounding. Disclose missing, anomalous, or inconsistent data. Do not silently impute values or combine mismatched units, periods, or definitions.

Use charts to clarify a comparison or trend. Label title, axes, units, period, and source. Avoid misleading proportions, truncated axes, or omitted context. State what the chart can and cannot show. Use methods that fit the assignment and course material; ask when uncertain instead of introducing complex methods without basis.

### Presentations

Confirm audience, duration, slide limit, responsibilities, and Rubric. Give each slide one main message and a coherent narrative. Link facts, figures, and charts to verifiable sources; check slide citations and the reference slide. Work only on the requested portion; do not automatically create a complete deck or submit it. Credit images, charts, and examples and follow applicable permissions and course rules.
